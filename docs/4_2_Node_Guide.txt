# The 2-Node 4-Step Theory: An Architectural Guide for Claude

**Purpose:** This document explains the "2-Node 4-Step Theory" architecture to Claude, ensuring alignment with the system's developmental roadmap. It details the dual-node cognitive structure (Logic vs. Symbolic) and the four questions as structural scaffolding — starting coordinates organized around natural questions of self-discovery, with seed URLs where both logical and symbolic content naturally exist.

---

## 1. The Core Architecture: Dual-Node Cognition

The system eschews unified processing in favor of two distinct, specialized nodes mediated by a Bridge Memory.

* **Logic Node (The Anchor):** Processes verifiable facts, causal relationships, and structural data. It requires high-confidence corroboration and rejects ambiguity.
* **Symbolic Node (The Kite):** Processes metaphors, emotional resonance, and cultural narratives. It is initially dormant to prevent hallucination but activates later to provide meaning.
* **Bridge Memory (The Arbiter):** A controlled buffer where inputs are evaluated. It uses **Chaos-Regularized Optimization (CRO)** to weigh factual validity against symbolic resonance before routing data to the appropriate node.
